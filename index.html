ProbeCheck study

Consent to participate

You are invited to take part in a study of ProbeCheck, a research prototype that reveals the seams in linear probe experiments. Seams are “mismatches and cracks” between the assumptions made when building an AI system and the reality of its use (Ehsan et al., 2024). We want to learn how well it helps researchers notice and act on these seams.

What you will do

  1. Read five seams in linear probe experiments, one at a time.
  2. Explore the ProbeCheck prototype for up to minutes while thinking aloud. You can finish earlier.
  3. Answer open questions aloud about how well the prototype addresses the seams.

The session takes about minutes in total.

Recording and your information

The session is recorded so that it can be transcribed. Everything you say is anonymized in the transcript. The only personal information kept is your experience in the mechanistic interpretability field, your age, and your gender.

Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question, or stop at any time without giving a reason.
