probecheck

ProbeCheck reveals the seams (“mismatches and cracks” between the assumptions made when building an AI system and the reality of its use; Ehsan et al., 2024) in linear probe experiments: the gaps between what an experiment reports and what can actually be checked. It shows what is missing to rerun an experiment, reruns it to see whether the results hold, and tests alternative hypotheses on new data to see whether the probe detects what the paper claims. Built for mechanistic interpretability researchers.

How it works

  1. You are hereChoose a linear probe research paper to inspect
  2. Analyze how reproducible are the outlined linear probes experiments
  1. Reproduce the experiment and test alternative hypotheses on new data
